# C. glabrata Hermes, micafungin tolerance and persistence (PRJNA1304976)

Hermes Tn-seq of *C. glabrata* BG2 induction 3 at three micafungin concentrations (15, 32 and 64 ng/mL), each
sampled at 0 h (control) and 6 h (treated). Johns Hopkins deposit; no publication at the time of writing, so it is
used for QC, density and method behaviour, never as a published comparison.

One pool (`BG2_Ind3`) holds all three concentrations, so the three 0 h cultures are three controls of one pool: the
z-score, resampling and the multi-condition series are all available.

Read structure and reference as the other Cunningham-laboratory sets (`hermes` / `splinkerette_pcr`,
`resources/species/glabrata`).

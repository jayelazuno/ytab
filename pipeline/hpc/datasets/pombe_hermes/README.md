# S. pombe Hermes libraries

- Guo et al. 2013 (SRR327340): in vivo Hermes; 50-bp reads with only the last 10 bp of the Hermes end (`terminal_10bp`). The library Segal et al. 2018 used; its published hits are in `resources/species/pombe/tnseq_data/guo_2013_hermes`.
- SRR836165: Hermes transposition into naked DNA in vitro, not a fitness library (`include` False).
- Grech et al. 2019, Mol Biol Evol (PRJEB27324): 16 high-density Hermes libraries, paired-end; R1 is the junction mate (`end_ligated_adapter`).
- Levin lab 2020 (PRJNA517661): WT and otr1R::ura4 strains at generation 0 (control) and 80 (treated) (`end_ligated_adapter`).

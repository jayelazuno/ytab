# Yao et al. 2026 - Komagataella phaffii piggyBac (PRJNA1376236)

piggyBac insertion library of *K. phaffii* (Pichia pastoris) GS115 and two later passages; Yao X et al. (2026)
J Fungi 12:136, doi:10.3390/jof12020136 (position-effect variegation).

Three runs in one pool (`G15P`): the library (role parent) and passages 4 and 67. Small, and included for the
essentiality and density comparison rather than for its conditions.

Read structure: 250 bp paired end, R1 carries the junction (`piggybac` / `pb_tr_end`; 94% of the first 2,000 reads of
SRR36343507 begin with the terminal repeat; 33% of the trimmed reads map at MAPQ >= 20, the lowest of the piggyBac
sets, so its QC must be read before it is used).

Reference: `resources/species/phaffii` (GCF_000027005.1 ASM2700v1, GS115; 5,040 protein-coding genes).
